# Camera and continuity in Studio

Keep a stable overview while the viewer learns the mechanism. Move closer only
when the important internal change is too small to read. Name the reason, the
outgoing and incoming subjects and a settled readable interval (at least 200ms).
Carry actor identity, quantity and geography explicitly at scene boundaries.
If the incoming geography differs, use an honest settled cut instead of claiming
a continuous carry. Sample both sides of every boundary and playback around it.

Choose `speaker:"page"` during dense action. Human narration continues even
when the speaker is absent. Before returning the speaker, check that the
presenter and captions do not cover the trigger, changed quantity or destination.
Generated-only scenes reserve no presenter space. Inspect the exact composed
frames returned by Studio, including the shot plan; an isolated object still is
not evidence of safe placement in the video.

These are Studio-native instructions authored for this repair; no upstream
camera/player source is incorporated by this file.
