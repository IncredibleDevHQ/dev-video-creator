# Coach

**Before the take — framing check.** From the camera: face width (target 0.14 W chest-up), headroom 8–12 %, eye row ≈ 0.38, luminance and skin band, background energy, mic level. One plain cue each ("sit a little higher", "your background is busy: information will sit beside you").

**Five rules shown once.** Open on you · hand the frame to the material when it needs it · one change per idea · never talk over a change · come back to close.

**During the take.** Program view (un-mirrored in the gesture family), the current family, the next planned change 2 s ahead with a countdown, ≤ 3 offers keyed 1·2·3 (planned, alternates, escape hatches) shown only when legal (dwell ≥ 4 s, not mid-word or mid-reframe, presence floor, information safety). Cues in the presenter's own left/right; never recorded. Modes: off / assist / auto.

**After the take — report.** Layout use over time, dwell violations, moments text fell under 18 px, moments a speaking person was hidden, escape hatches used vs the track, the three highest-gain improvements.
